Parking information,

Parkaris helps drivers exchange time-sensitive information about public parking spaces that may become available. It is not a parking operator and does not sell, rent, reserve, block or guarantee any public parking space.

Important

Every driver remains responsible for checking road signs, local rules, access restrictions, fees and the actual availability of a space before parking.
