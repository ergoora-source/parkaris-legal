Privacy Policy — English

Data controller: Aleksandar Nenadovic, Greece · parkaris.dev@gmail.com
App: Parkaris · Android package: com.parkpeer.app

1. Role of the service

Parkaris is a digital intermediary for exchanging time-sensitive information between drivers about public parking spaces that may become available. It does not own, operate or manage parking facilities. It does not sell, rent, reserve, block or guarantee any public parking space.

2. Data we may process

  • account identifier, display name, verified phone number and an optional linked Google recovery account,
  • vehicle information entered by the user; a full registration plate is not intended for public display and only limited details are shown to other users,
  • precise location when required to publish information, search, estimate distance or support an active exchange,
  • background location only during a user-initiated function made visible by a notification, such as shared arrival progress during an active exchange,
  • publication, request, exchange, confirmation, Smart session and related timestamp information,
  • internal P-unit balance and transactions, Trial/Premium status and limited Google Play purchase-verification information,
  • push token, language preference, security, App Check, diagnostic, performance and usage-event information.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to provide user-requested functions, protect accounts, prevent abuse and fraud, provide support, resolve disputes, improve reliability and meet legal obligations. Depending on the context, processing relies on performance of the service, consent or device permission, legitimate security interests, or a legal obligation.

4. Sharing and service providers

Google and Firebase services are used for authentication, databases, notifications, backend functions, integrity checks, maps, navigation, analytics and Google Play purchases. Limited exchange information is shown only to relevant participants when necessary for the function. We do not sell personal data.

5. Retention

Active data is kept for as long as needed for the account and service. After deletion, personal identifiers are removed or disconnected unless limited retention is required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, transactions, disputes or legal compliance. Analytics linked only to an anonymous installation identifier may be retained for up to 365 days.

6. Choices and rights

Users may withdraw device permissions through Android settings, correct available profile information, request access or deletion, and delete the account from the app. Withdrawing location permission may disable functions that require it. Privacy requests may be sent to parkaris.dev@gmail.com.

7. Security and children

Technical and organizational safeguards are used to limit unauthorized access, but absolute security cannot be promised. The service is intended for drivers legally entitled to drive and is not directed to children.
