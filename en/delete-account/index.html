Delete within the app

  1. Open Parkaris and Profile.
  2. Select Account security.
  3. Select Permanently delete Parkaris account.
  4. Complete secure verification and confirm the final warning.

Deletion may be postponed while an exchange is active so that it can end safely.

Request deletion without app access

Email parkaris.dev@gmail.com with the subject Parkaris account deletion request. Include the account phone number, display name if known, and a clear deletion request. Do not send an SMS code, Google code, password or device code. Additional secure verification may be required.

Data deleted

  • authentication account and active profile,
  • phone, linked access and vehicles,
  • notifications, messaging token and private settings,
  • active P balance, profile transactions and Trial/Premium entitlement,
  • active publications, exchanges, Smart sessions and matches.

Limited retention

Some records may be retained or anonymized where required for security, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, Google Play transactions or a legal obligation. Google Play independently manages payment and subscription records.

Deleting the account or app does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Parkaris.
